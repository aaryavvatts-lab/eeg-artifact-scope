> **Why no precision column.** Subjects blink spontaneously between cues, and those detections are correct. Scoring them as false positives would understate the detector; without frame-by-frame video annotation, recall and detections-per-cue are what the data supports. A detections-per-cue near 1.00 is the calibration target.
